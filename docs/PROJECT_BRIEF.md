# Project Brief — Video Discovery, Product Search & Digital Advertising Platform

Client/owner: Alfabiz.AI. Market: Kenya, expanding to wider Africa. Proposal approved Aug 2026; project is now in active development.

Read this before starting any task on this project. For execution detail (stack, sprints, team), see [DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md](DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md).

## What this is

A video-first search, product discovery, business directory and advertising ecosystem — part search engine, part short-form video network, part business directory, part digital advertising platform. Video is the primary content medium throughout, not an add-on.

Strategic line from the proposal: **Search. Discover. Watch. Compare. Connect. Advertise.**

## The owner's stated priority

The owner is particular about UI/UX: the product must feel like a **modern, highly interactive** consumer app — not a generic admin-style CRUD interface. Every screen the Frontend Engineer and UI/UX Designer touch should be held to that bar, especially anything user-facing (search results, video feed, product pages, comparison).

## Core modules (from the approved proposal)

- **Search & Discovery Engine** — the entry point; search spans products, companies, services, videos, articles, books/journals, manufacturers, suppliers, education content.
- **Video & Shorts Platform** — product videos, manufacturing/"how it's made" videos, demos, DIY/education content, a Shorts-style vertical feed for habitual engagement.
- **Business & Company Management** — verified company profiles acting as a digital showroom (logo, cover video, description, industry, locations, contacts, products, verification status).
- **Product & Service Management** — listings with specs, images/video, price, manufacturer/supplier, warranty, availability, related products.
- **Product Comparison** — side-by-side comparison on price, specs, features, manufacturer, warranty, location, availability, ratings.
- **Recommendation Engine** — surfaces related content/products from search terms, interests, watch/view history, location, trending content.
- **Content Moderation** — all UGC and commercial content reviewed before publication; AI-assisted flagging planned for later phases.
- **Books, Journals & Publications** — a dedicated indexed section.
- **Education & Knowledge** — free educational content across science, tech, agriculture, health, business, DIY.
- **Location-Based Discovery** — find nearby businesses/suppliers; location doubles as an ad-targeting parameter.
- **Digital Advertising** — sponsored search, sponsored videos, featured products/companies, location- and category-based targeting, full campaigns. All sponsored placements are clearly labelled and kept distinct from organic results.
- **Advertising Analytics** — impressions, video views, search appearances, clicks, leads, engagement, campaign performance.
- **Content Creator Ecosystem** — creator profiles, submissions, licensing/rights, contracts, payments.
- **Administration** — centralised backend control across users, businesses, products, videos, categories, ads, moderation, creators, publications, reports, payments, analytics, with configurable admin roles.

## User roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Platform Administrator | Full system management, configuration, oversight |
| Content Moderator | Reviews, approves, rejects uploaded content |
| Business Administrator | Manages a company's profile, products, services, content |
| Advertiser | Creates, funds, manages ad campaigns |
| Content Creator | Uploads content for review and publication |
| Publisher | Manages books, journals, publications |
| General User | Searches, watches, compares, discovers — free of charge |

## Business model

General use is free; monetisation is business/advertiser-side: sponsored search, sponsored videos, featured products, featured company profiles, advertising campaigns, creator partnerships, physical (out-of-home) advertising, and premium business services (advanced analytics, premium pages, lead-gen tools).

## Content categories (launch set, admin-extensible)

Manufacturing, Agriculture, Education, Health, Technology, Science, Construction, Automotive, Retail, Beauty & Lifestyle, Energy, Finance, Hospitality, Arts & Culture, Books & Journals, Entertainment, DIY, Professional Services.

## Roadmap beyond Phase 1

- **Phase 2** — advanced recommendations, personalised feeds, AI-powered search, advanced comparison/advertising/analytics, creator management, AI-assisted moderation.
- **Phase 3** — voice & natural-language search, AI-driven discovery, physical/out-of-home advertising (stadiums, exhibitions, billboards) as a distinct business line requiring venue agreements and a field sales capability.
