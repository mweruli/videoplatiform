Design notes for PM review

  • Status: Draft — ready for first-pass review, not yet approved for build.
  • Sponsored vs Featured: amber is used consistently for anything paid — "Sponsored" in search/feed, "Featured" on home showroom row — per the palette's stated role for accent color.
  • No real photography: thumbnails use brand-gradient + icon in place of product photos/video frames, since none exist yet. Swap 1:1 once real assets land.
  • Verification states: teal check = verified, grey clock = pending review. Search "construction" or "hardware" to see the pending state (BuildRight Hardware).
  • Compare is modelled as a persistent cross-screen tray (add from any product card, up to 3) rather than a dedicated page — confirm this matches intent vs. a full comparison screen.
  • Account tab and location picker are stubbed with a toast — onboarding/auth is a separate flow per the brief.
  • Try the suggested searches, scroll the video feed, and tap into a business to see the full loop.
